Overall day state

Built from what you actually notice

—/10

You do not need to choose a mood number. A logged day starts around your normal baseline, then motivation, energy, capacity, enjoyment, emotional tone, symptoms, and other signals gently move the estimate.

Emotional toneYour general emotional “weather,” separate from one anxious or irritating moment.
Motivation / driveWhen you actually had the opportunity, how pulled did you feel to start or engage with things? Not how much you accomplished or how ambitious your earlier plans were.
Energy / fuelHow awake, activated, and mentally/physically fueled did you feel? Low can mean tired, sluggish, sleepy, or mentally slow.
Capacity / bandwidthHow much demand, interruption, frustration, or input could you comfortably handle before it felt like too much?
Enjoyment / sparkHow available did interest, pleasure, and “that sounds fun” feel? Doing a hobby is not required; what matters is whether things felt appealing or rewarding.

Don’t rate these based on how productive you were. You can do a lot with low motivation, want to do a lot with low capacity, or have plenty of energy while having very little bandwidth for people/problems.

Help me decide cheat sheet + 4 quick questions
Motivation / driveLow = little pull to start · Typical = normal drive · High = actively eager to engage/do things.
Energy / fuelLow = tired/sluggish/mentally slow · Typical = normal fuel · High = especially alert/activated/energetic.
Capacity / bandwidthLow = normal requests or interruptions feel like “one more thing” · Typical = ordinary demands manageable · High = noticeably more room than usual.
Enjoyment / sparkLow = things seem meh/unrewarding · Typical = usual enjoyment · High = things feel especially appealing or rewarding.
Examples from my real life

Chores feel overwhelming + leisure feels impossible + I doomscroll: Low motivation + Low capacity.

Chores feel like too much, so I intentionally let myself rest: Low motivation + Low capacity. Choosing rest is the healthier response; the underlying bandwidth can still be low.

I don’t want to do chores, but I do them and they’re manageable: Low motivation + Typical capacity.

I’m tired/sluggish, but an interruption or request doesn’t bother me much: Low energy + Typical/High capacity.

I’m awake/energized, but coworkers needing things makes me think “please leave me alone”: Typical/High energy + Low capacity.

Crochet/gaming still sounds fun, but starting it feels like effort: Low motivation + Typical enjoyment.

Nothing sounds appealing — gaming, crochet, shows, projects all feel meh: Low enjoyment.

I didn’t want to start gaming/crochet, but once I did I had a great time: Low motivation + Typical/High enjoyment.

I start chores and naturally keep going / clean extra: likely High capacity; motivation may be Typical or High depending on whether the drive was there before you started or arrived with momentum.

1. Drive: When I actually had the chance to do things, did I feel pulled to start?
2. Fuel: How awake / activated did I feel?
3. Bandwidth: How did extra requests, interruptions, or problems feel?
4. Spark: Did normally enjoyable things sound or feel appealing?
