What the guided tools are adding

Data

Backup and restore

Export a JSON backup to keep your entries safe or move them to another device.

Bring your history

Import Bearable CSV

Import your existing Bearable export so symptoms, factors, sleep, notes, and functioning can appear on the calendar. Health-metric-only days are ignored. Old Bearable mood numbers are preserved but excluded; Inner Compass rebuilds those days from the other emotional signals you logged.

The importer adds or merges days; it does not erase your Inner Compass reflections.

Privacy

Stored on this device

Inner Compass has no account, server, tracking pixel, or cloud database. Clearing browser storage can remove tracker data and reflections, so export backups occasionally.

Reset

Delete all app data

This permanently removes every saved check-in and discovery from this browser.

A note about support

This is a reflection tool

It cannot diagnose mental-health conditions or replace professional care. If an exercise makes you feel worse, pause and choose a grounding activity or trusted support person.
